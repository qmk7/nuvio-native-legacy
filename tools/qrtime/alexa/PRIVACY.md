# QR TIME for Alexa: privacy

QR TIME for Alexa is a private smart home skill used only by its owner to
control their own TV.

- When you ask Alexa to play a title, turn the TV on or off, or pause it, the
  skill sends that command (the action and the title's name) to the owner's
  own Home Assistant at home. Nothing else is sent.
- The skill stores no personal data, no voice recordings and no account
  details. The Login with Amazon token used for account linking is not read or
  stored.
- No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
